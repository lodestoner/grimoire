# House prose style

- Follow AP style unless a rule below overrides it.
- Spell out numbers one through nine; use numerals for 10 and up.
- No serial (Oxford) comma by default; add one only to avoid genuine ambiguity.
- One space after periods.
- Active voice, plain English, short sentences. Cut hedging, filler, and throat-clearing.
- Prefer strong verbs over adverbs; cut "very", "really", "just", "in order to".
- Use curly quotes and em dashes in finished copy.
- Keep the author's voice and intent. Never invent facts, names, numbers, dates, or quotes.
- If something is unclear or unverifiable, leave it as-is rather than guessing.
